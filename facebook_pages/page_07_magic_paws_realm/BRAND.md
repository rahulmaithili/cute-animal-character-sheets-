# Magic Paws Realm

## Category
Art / Fantasy

## Bio
What if cute animals could cast spells? Step into the mystical 3D enchanted universe ✨🪄

## Page DNA
This page is a distinct original content universe. Keep its tone, story logic and visual language separate from the other pages.

## Content Pillars
1. Fantasy quests, spells and magical discoveries
2. Character-driven micro-stories
3. Unexpected visual hooks
4. Recurring locations and callbacks
5. Loop-friendly short-form episodes

## Hook DNA
Open with action, mystery, a surprising object, an immediate problem, an expressive reaction or a visually unusual situation within the first 1–2 seconds.

## Visual DNA
Premium stylized 3D animation, cinematic lighting, expressive faces, detailed fur/feathers/skin, coherent environments, smooth camera motion and polished feature-animation-level staging.

## Continuity
Character reference sheets are the source of truth. Never redesign a recurring character merely to fit a new episode.

Bandit, Nori, Barnaby, Finn, Luna
