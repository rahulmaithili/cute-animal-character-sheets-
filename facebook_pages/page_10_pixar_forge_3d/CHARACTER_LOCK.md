# Pixar Forge 3D — Character Lock

## Character Source of Truth
Select a character from the repository and provide its exact reference image to the generation system. The supplied reference image controls identity.

## Required Character Preservation
- Exact species
- Exact face structure
- Exact eye shape and color
- Exact fur, skin, shell or feather pattern
- Exact body proportions
- Exact signature accessories or visual cues
- Same recognizable silhouette

## Allowed Episode Changes
Costume, pose, environment, lighting and episode-specific props may change when the concept requires them. Character identity may not change.

## Design Showcase Rule
When demonstrating a transformation, change only the intended design variable. Keep the base character recognizable before, during and after the transformation.

## Negative Continuity
No species swap, face redesign, eye drift, anatomy mutation, extra limbs, duplicate character, random accessory replacement, age change, identity blending or inconsistent proportions.
