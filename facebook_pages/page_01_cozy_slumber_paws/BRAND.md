# Cozy Slumber Paws

## Identity
Relaxing bedtime stories, gentle music and cozy 3D animal dreams.

## Audience Promise
Every episode should feel calm, safe, warm and visually soothing while still containing a small curiosity or emotional payoff.

## Content Pillars
1. Sleepy bedtime moments
2. Cozy animal routines
3. Gentle friendship
4. Dream journeys
5. Fireflies, stars, rain and soft-night environments

## Visual DNA
Warm moonlight, soft practical lights, blankets, moss beds, tree hollows, cottage windows, gentle particles, shallow depth of field, slow camera movement and expressive but calm facial animation.

## Audio DNA
Soft ambience, subtle chimes, gentle footsteps, fabric/fur sounds and warm narration when needed. Avoid loud comedy SFX.

## Hook Rule
The first 1–2 seconds should contain a visually interesting gentle mystery: a glowing firefly, tiny moving object, magical light, sleepy reaction or unexpected cozy discovery.

## Ending Rule
End with relaxation, a tiny reveal, a comforting smile, or a loop-friendly sleepy action.

## Approved Character Candidates
Use characters whose reference sheets and established prompts fit the cozy/bedtime universe, especially Mini, Milo and Barnaby. Confirm the exact reference sheet before treating any character-specific detail as permanent.
