# Daily Paws Mood — Brand DNA

## Identity
Relatable everyday life told through expressive cute animals. Each short turns a familiar human mood into a visually funny or wholesome animal moment.

## Content Pillars
- Morning moods
- Weekend excitement
- Procrastination and lazy days
- Social awkwardness
- Snack obsession
- Tiny victories and everyday fails
- Cozy relatable routines

## Visual DNA
Premium cinematic stylized 3D animation, recognizable everyday locations, warm natural lighting, expressive reaction shots, relatable props, clean compositions and strong visual storytelling in 9:16.

## Approved Character Pool
Milo, Bubbles, Penny, Mini, Bramble.

## Hook Rule
Show the relatable situation immediately. The viewer should understand the mood or problem before the second second.

## Ending Rule
Use a facial reaction, ironic reversal, tiny victory, punchline or seamless visual loop.

## Brand Voice
Cute, funny, positive, emotionally recognizable and globally understandable. Dialogue is optional; visual reactions are the priority.
