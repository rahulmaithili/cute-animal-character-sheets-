# STORY FORMAT BIBLE — All 10 Pages, All Characters

**Version 1.0 — 2026-09-28**
**Rule: No episode is shot without an approved film-plan first (logline → 3 acts → shot list).**

---

## UNIVERSAL STORY ENGINE (applies to every page)

Every 30-second episode is a complete mini-film in 3 acts:

| Act | Time | Job |
|-----|------|-----|
| **ACT 1 — HOOK + WANT** | 0–8s | Cold open inside the action in the first 2 seconds (no slow establishing shot). Establish the character and what they want — or the problem that just exploded. |
| **ACT 2 — OBSTACLE + TRY/FAIL** | 8–22s | Something blocks the want. Two attempts: first fails funny, second fails bigger (or twists). Stakes rise. |
| **ACT 3 — PAYOFF** | 22–30s | Clever solution, twist, or emotional button. The payoff must feel EARNED by Act 2. Page-signature ending. |

### Engagement Rules (non-negotiable, from audience feedback)
1. **0–2s cold open** — start mid-action, mid-surprise, or mid-reaction.
2. **Every shot carries a beat** — surprise, chase, comedy, or wonder. No filler.
3. **Big expressions** — gasp, giggle, pout, triumph. Faces sell retention.
4. **Dynamic camera** — crash zooms on reactions, whip-pans on chases, tracking on quests.
5. **Sound sells story** — hooks need a sound spike; comedy needs foley; payoffs need a music lift.

### Film-Plan Workflow (per episode)
1. **Film-plan** written: title, logline, 3-act beats, 3-shot list (camera + action + sound).
2. **User approves** the film-plan.
3. **Shoot**: 3 shots × ~10s, character reference locked, match-cut continuity.
4. **Stitch**: white-flash transitions, exactly 30.0s, upload to page Drive folder.

### Arc Structure (retention across episodes)
Every 5 episodes form a **mini-arc** — a continuing thread with a cliffhanger every 5th episode's ending teasing the next arc. Standalone episodes still work alone, but arc viewers binge.

## PAGE 1 — Cozy Slumber Paws
**Premise:** Bedtime is an adventure. Tiny Mini (and friends) turn every night into a playful quest that always ends in the coziest sleep.
**Cast & story roles:**
- **Mini** (pygmy marmoset) — the curious heroine; every quest starts with her wonder.
- **Milo** (sleepy golden puppy) — the sleepyhead; comedy from dozing off mid-quest; heart from his loyalty.
- **Barnaby** (snowy baby owl) — the wide-awake night guide; knows night secrets; deadpan comedy.

**Page story engine (3 acts, bedtime-flavored):**
- Act 1: Something disrupts bedtime (a sound, a glow, a lost blanket). Hook in 2s.
- Act 2: Playful night quest with 2 try/fail beats — chase, comedy, a small scare turned funny.
- Act 3: The coziest sleep imaginable — earned, warm, lullaby close.

**Episode formula:** "Bedtime disruption → playful quest → coziest sleep."
**Example film-plan — Ep11 "Mini and the Midnight Rainbow":** Logline: after night rain a moonbow appears; Mini slides down it, but the colors start fading mid-slide. Acts: (1) rain stops, moonbow ignites, Mini gasps; (2) slide + colors dull + she nearly slips, then sprinkles flower-crown petals to relight the colors; (3) full slide, lands giggling in her moss bed — it was a dream; wakes with a rainbow petal in her crown. Shots: crash-zoom reveal / dynamic slide + fix / slow pull-back dream wake.
**Arc idea (Ep11–15): "The Lost Rainbow Petal"** — the dream petal is real and fades a little each night; Mini must return it to the moonbow.

## PAGE 2 — Wild Sprout Adventures
**Premise:** Tiny paws, big world. Bandit and friends go on forest quests and uncover hidden wonders.
**Cast & story roles:**
- **Bandit** (raccoon) — the mischievous leader; schemes, gets the gang into trouble.
- **Finn** (fennec fox) — the scout; giant ears hear trouble first; brave but tiny.
- **Rusty** (red panda) — the muscle + comic relief; climbs anything, snacks always.
- **Clover** (lamb) — the gentle heart; kindness solves what strength can't.
- **Bramble** (young moose) — the clumsy giant; accidental destruction, accidental saves.

**Page story engine:**
- Act 1: A mysterious find or map/sound pulls the gang in. Hook: the discovery itself.
- Act 2: Quest with mishaps — wrong turns, a mini "villain" (grumpy badger, thorny bush), teamwork gag.
- Act 3: Hidden wonder revealed — glowing grove, secret waterfall; awe close.

**Episode formula:** "Mysterious find → quest with mishaps → hidden wonder."
**Example film-plan — "The Humming Stone":** Logline: Finn hears a stone humming underground; the gang digs, floods the tunnel by accident, and discovers a crystal cavern. Acts: (1) Finn's ears twitch at the hum, gang gathers; (2) dig chaos — Bramble's antlers collapse the tunnel, water rushes in, they surf out on a leaf; (3) the water drains into a glowing crystal cavern — pure wonder. Shots: ear-twitch close-up / dig-disaster tracking / cavern reveal crane-up.
**Arc idea (Ep1–5): "The Five Humming Stones"** — each stone hums a note; together they play a forest song.

## PAGE 3 — Goofy Paws 3D
**Premise:** Tiny paws, giant trouble. Pure comedy — clumsy animals, harmless chaos, accidental wins.
**Cast & story roles:**
- **Bubbles** (chubby hamster) — the snack-obsessed; everything is about food.
- **Bramble** (young moose) — the disaster magnet; breaks it, then fixes it worse.
- **Penny** (piglet) — the dramatic diva; overreacts to everything, secretly sweet.
- **Milo** (sleepy puppy) — sleeps through chaos; wakes at the punchline.
- **Rusty** (red panda) — the show-off; tricks always backfire.

**Page story engine:**
- Act 1: A simple plan is announced with total confidence. Hook: the confident claim.
- Act 2: Chaos cascade — plan fails, backup plan fails funnier, everything snowballs.
- Act 3: Accidental win — the mess itself solves the problem; freeze-frame celebration.

**Episode formula:** "Simple plan → chaos cascade → hilarious accidental win."
**Example film-plan — "Bubbles vs the Giant Strawberry":** Logline: Bubbles tries to carry a strawberry bigger than his head; it rolls away and chaos follows. Acts: (1) Bubbles declares "I got this"; (2) the strawberry rolls downhill, Bramble trips over it, Penny gets splashed with juice, Milo naps through it all; (3) the strawberry lands perfectly sliced on a leaf — instant picnic; everyone cheers, Milo wakes for the food. Shots: confident declaration close-up / rolling-chaos tracking / picnic freeze-frame.
**Arc idea:** "The Chaos Cup" — weekly tournament of who causes the funniest disaster.

## PAGE 4 — Heart Paws Tales
**Premise:** Heartwarming 3D stories of friendship, love and belonging. Wholesome, soul-touching.
**Cast & story roles:**
- **Clover** (lamb) — the kindness engine; notices who is sad.
- **Pip** (platypus) — the quirky helper; weird ideas that somehow work.
- **Pebble** (sea turtle) — the slow wise one; patience as a superpower.
- **Milo** (sleepy puppy) — the comforter; his sleepy warmth heals.
- **Penny** (piglet) — the one learning kindness; small growth every story.

**Page story engine:**
- Act 1: Someone is sad/lost/left out. Hook: the emotion, readable in 2s.
- Act 2: Kind attempts — first try flops (gentle comedy), second try needs courage.
- Act 3: Emotional payoff — hug, reunion, belonging; warm tear-jerker button.

**Episode formula:** "Friend in need → kind tries → heart-melting payoff."
**Example film-plan — "Pebble's Slow Parade":** Logline: Pebble is too slow for the pond parade; Clover organizes a parade at Pebble's pace. Acts: (1) Pebble watches the fast parade, left behind, eyes droop; (2) Clover's fast plan fails — everyone rushes past again; she slows everyone down with flower chains; (3) the whole pond parades at turtle pace behind Pebble, who leads beaming — slowest float wins loudest cheers. Shots: drooping-eyes close-up / flower-chain slowdown / parade wide, warm glow.
**Arc idea (Ep1–5): "The Kindness Garden"** — each good deed plants a flower; the garden blooms in the finale.

## PAGE 5 — Tiny Scholars 3D
**Premise:** Fun animal facts and life lessons, brought to life by adorable buddies. Learn + laugh.
**Cast & story roles:**
- **Barnaby** (snowy owl) — the professor; asks the big question, hosts the lesson.
- **Nori** (dart frog) — the lab assistant; tiny, fearless, jumps into experiments.
- **Pebble** (sea turtle) — the field researcher; slow but thorough.
- **Finn** (fennec fox) — the skeptic; "prove it!" energy, won over by demo.
- **Clover** (lamb) — the student surrogate; asks what the viewer wonders.

**Page story engine:**
- Act 1: A big curious question + a wild guess. Hook: the question as a visual riddle.
- Act 2: Funny experiments — two demos, first goes wrong (comedy), second reveals the truth.
- Act 3: The "wow" fact landed simply + cute recap visual.

**Episode formula:** "Big question → funny experiments → wow fact."
**Example film-plan — "Why Do Frogs Sing in the Rain?":** Logline: Nori croaks before rain; the gang tests if frogs are weather wizards. Acts: (1) Nori croaks, clouds gather — coincidence?; (2) experiment 1: Finn splashes water on Nori — she croaks angrily (comedy); experiment 2: Barnaby shows real rain coming, Nori's throat pouch puffs — she sensed the humidity; (3) wow fact: frogs sense rain through their skin; Nori takes a bow in the drizzle. Shots: croak + clouds / splash-gag + humidity demo / bow in rain.
**Arc idea:** "The Five Senses Lab" — one sense per episode, finale quiz episode.

## PAGE 6 — Fluffy ASMR 3D
**Premise:** Satisfying 3D animal sounds, gentle fur brushing and sensory relaxation. Story-light, sensation-heavy.
**Cast & story roles:**
- **Milo** (sleepy puppy) — the bliss model; melting expressions sell the tingle.
- **Mini** (marmoset) — the tiny masseuse; delicate fingers, perfect for detail sounds.
- **Rusty** (red panda) — the fluffy giant; tail-brushing, big satisfying strokes.
- **Pip** (platypus) — the water artist; splashes, droplets, smooth bill slides.
- **Barnaby** (owl) — the feather whisperer; soft hoots, downy preening.

**Page story engine (sensory journey, not plot):**
- Act 1: The texture/object is revealed with a teaser sound. Hook: the sound itself in 2s.
- Act 2: Sensory escalation — layering sounds (brushing → tapping → water), close-up macros.
- Act 3: The ultimate satisfying release — the big brush-out, the perfect splash, bliss face.

**Episode formula:** "Tingle setup → sensory escalation → deeply satisfying release."
**Example film-plan — "Rusty's Tail Brush-Out":** Logline: Rusty's striped tail is full of forest tangles; Mini gives it the ultimate brush-out. Acts: (1) close-up: tangled tail, first brush stroke sound; (2) brushing layers — soft, then rhythmic, leaves and fluff flying in slow-mo; (3) the final smooth stroke — tail fluffs to perfect stripes, Rusty melts with bliss. Shots: macro tangle / slow-mo fluff storm / bliss-face finale.
**Arc idea:** "The Five Textures" — fur, feather, scale, fluff, water; one per episode.

**Premise:** What if cute animals could cast spells? A mystical enchanted universe of fantasy quests.
**Cast & story roles:**
- **Bandit** (raccoon) — the rogue mage; steals spellbooks, improvises badly.
- **Nori** (dart frog) — the potion imp; tiny cauldron, bubbling brews.

- **Finn** (fennec fox) — the apprentice; eager, spells half-work.

**Page story engine:**
- Act 1: A spell is cast or a magical object found. Hook: the magic misfiring in 2s.
- Act 2: Spell backfires escalate — two magical mishaps, each weirder; the gang scrambles.
- Act 3: The mishap becomes the magic — backfire turns out to be exactly what was needed; enchanted tableau.

**Episode formula:** "Spell attempt → magical mishap → enchanted payoff."
**Example film-plan — "Finn's Ears Hear Magic":** Logline: Finn tries a hearing spell to find the lost Moon Acorn; he hears EVERYTHING at once. Acts: (1) Finn casts, ears balloon with sound waves — comedic overload; (2) he follows the wrong sounds — a snoring badger, a arguing squirrels — chaos; (3) he filters to the faintest tinkle — the Moon Acorn, hidden in plain sight; it chimes and blooms with light. Shots: ear-balloon close-up / wrong-sound chase / acorn bloom wide.
**Arc idea (Ep1–5): "The Shattered Moon Acorn"** — five shards, five spells, finale restores it.

## PAGE 9 — Daily Paws Mood
**Premise:** Relatable everyday life told through expressive cute animals. Human moods, animal faces.
**Cast & story roles:**

**Page story engine:**
- Act 1: The relatable situation, instantly readable. Hook: the mood in under 2s.
- Act 2: Mood escalation — it gets worse/funnier in two beats; viewer sees themselves.
- Act 3: Reaction punchline — ironic reversal, tiny victory, or seamless loop.

**Episode formula:** "Relatable setup → mood escalation → reaction punchline."

Shots: alarm slap / snooze montage / blanket-cape sprint.
**Arc idea:** "A Week of Moods" — Monday to Sunday, one mood per episode.

## CROSS-PAGE RULES (never break)
1. **Character lock is absolute** — reference sheet is visual law on every page.
2. **Never mix page DNA** — Page 3's chaos never leaks into Page 4's tenderness.
3. **Film-plan before shoot** — every episode, every page, no exceptions.
4. **Engagement rules apply everywhere** — even ASMR and showcase pages hook in 2s.
5. **No silent lookalike substitution** — if a character has no sheet, they don't appear.
